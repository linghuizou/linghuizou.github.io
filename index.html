01 / About

About Me

I am an assistant professor at the College of Pharmacy, Hunan University of Chinese Medicine. I received my Ph.D. in Chinese Materia Medica Pharmaceutics from Sichuan University.

My academic interests lie at the interface of pharmaceutical sciences, nanotechnology, natural products, and tumor immunology. My work explores how nanocarrier engineering and targeted regulation of the tumor microenvironment can improve the delivery and therapeutic performance of bioactive compounds.

I value mechanism-oriented research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the translation of pharmaceutical innovations into reproducible and clinically relevant strategies.

02 / Research

Research Interests

Our broad goal is to understand and engineer the interactions between therapeutic materials, the tumor microenvironment, and antitumor immunity.

Nanomedicine & Targeted Delivery

Design of nanocarriers and delivery systems to improve the stability, biodistribution, targeting, and therapeutic performance of bioactive compounds.

Cancer-Associated Fibroblasts

Understanding cancer-associated fibroblast (CAF) heterogeneity, plasticity, and their roles in shaping the tumor microenvironment.

Cancer Immunotherapy

Exploring strategies to remodel immunosuppressive tumor environments and support T-cell-mediated antitumor responses.

Natural Product Nanotechnology

Investigating natural product-derived bioactive systems and nanotechnology-enabled approaches for pharmaceutical applications.
